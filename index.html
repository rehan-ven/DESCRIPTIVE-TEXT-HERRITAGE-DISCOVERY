Part A · Descriptive Text Framework

What Makes a Descriptive Text?

Definition: A descriptive text is a text that describes a specific person, place, animal, or thing. Its communicative purpose is to give the reader a clear, vivid picture of a particular subject.

Generic Structure

1. Identification: This part introduces the object that is being described (what it is and where it is located).

2. Description: This part provides details about the object's characteristics, features, qualities, and other information.

Language Features
  • Simple Present Tense & Passive Voice: "The temple has several levels." / "Its walls are covered with detailed carvings."
  • Specific Nouns & Spatial Prepositions: "Borobudur Temple is near Magelang." / "along the terraces"
  • Adjectives: ancient, historical, grand, impressive, detailed, valuable
Part B & C · Interactive Model Passage Explore Highlights

Borobudur Temple

Borobudur Temple is an ancient Buddhist monument located in Central Java, Indonesia. Standing near the town of Magelang, it was built in the ninth century and is regarded as the largest Buddhist temple in the world. Every year, many visitors travel there to see it.

The monument is made of stone and has nine levels that rise like a giant pyramid. Its walls are covered with detailed carvings, and hundreds of statues of Buddha sit along the terraces. Visitors are allowed to walk around each level and admire the impressive architecture. As a valuable historical and cultural heritage site, Borobudur must be preserved so that future generations can still enjoy it.

Identification (Para 1) Description (Para 2) Dotted words: click for meaning
Text Structure Breakdown:
Paragraph 1 (Identification): Introduces Borobudur Temple, identifies it as an ancient Buddhist monument in Central Java near Magelang, mentions when it was built (9th century), and notes its fame.
Paragraph 2 (Description): Describes physical features (stone material, nine pyramid-like levels, detailed carvings, Buddha statues along terraces) and explains why this historical and cultural heritage must be preserved.

How an Expert Solves a TKA Question

Part D · Three TKA Reading Comprehension Strategies
Strategy 1 · Textual
Spot → Locate → Pick

Find facts stated directly in the text.
Example: Where is Borobudur Temple located? Search for place keywords (located, Central Java, Magelang).

Strategy 2 · Inferential
Clues → Link → Conclude

Connect clues across sentences.
Example: What can we infer about its importance? Link historical age, cultural heritage, and preservation.

Strategy 3 · Evidence
Read → Check → Decide

Verify each option against exact sentences.
Example: Which statement is best supported? Reject opinions; pick only what the text proves.

Prambanan Temple

Prambanan Temple is one of the biggest Hindu temple complexes in Indonesia. It stands in Central Java, near Yogyakarta. The temple is famous for its tall and beautiful buildings, and it is also known for its detailed stone carvings.

The complex has three main temples dedicated to Hindu gods. Many visitors come to enjoy its impressive architecture and to learn about Indonesian history and culture. Prambanan Temple is a significant part of Indonesia's cultural heritage. For this reason, it should be protected and preserved for future generations.

Ratu Boko Heritage Complex

Ratu Boko is an archaeological site standing on a limestone plateau three kilometers south of Prambanan Temple in Yogyakarta. Unlike nearby temples built purely for worship, Ratu Boko preserves stone gates, bathing pools, and defensive walls from an ancient royal settlement.

Because the hilltop overlooks both Mount Merapi and the Prambanan plain, historians believe the site served both ceremonial and strategic roles. Protecting its fragile stone terraces helps preserve evidence of Java's early kingdoms.
